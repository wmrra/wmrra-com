**Register Today:** https://www.motorsportreg.com/events/wmrra-2026-awards-banquet-lemay-americas-car-museum-580937

Each year, we get to spend one special night celebrating the ups and downs, the battles, the friendships, the wins and losses we had over the entire season.  We get to spend time with those that helped us, encouraged us, taught us, supported us along the way — it is a time without track distractions, bike issues, and where we get to just "be". Cheer on those that won, our club’s members, volunteers, sponsors, staff with good food & drink!   

This year, we are going to LeMay, “America’s Car Museum” and all are invited!

If you have never been to LeMay, it’s a pretty amazing space — feel free to go early, check out the museum (4 floors), and then join us that evening for our party! Parking is included!

**Important note:** Tickets to the museum are **NOT** included in our dinner.
