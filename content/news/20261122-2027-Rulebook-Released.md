**The 2027 WMRRA [Rulebook](/race/rulebook) and [Schedule](/race/schedule) are Released!**

WMRRA is excited to announce we are ready to share the schedule and rulebook for 2027. Below you will find a letter from our race referee:

---

WMRRA Members,

This year was my 3rd year managing the Rules Committee and was the largest by far, with a record 52 proposals (21 from members and 31 from Paul and me)—more than double our usual 20. It’s great to see the increase in club engagement. Your submissions and ideas are how we make the club into something we all can appreciate. While Paul and I are the largest group of submissions, that is done from feedback from all of you throughout the season.  That’s not to say you shouldn’t submit your proposal directly.  You have better context of what specifically you are needing from the club as an individual.  We appreciate your continued input into how we run the club and hope to see further engagement between the board and the members in the future. 

I’m including some of the more significant changes below to be sure everyone is made aware of them.  Please review the rulebook's revisions section for the complete list.

**Ninja 250:** A rulebook error in past years omitted a race fuel exclusion. This has been corrected. Bikes tuned for race fuel will need retuning.

**Yamaha R6 and R3:** Review the rulebook for a new safety wire requirement on these models.

**Number Plates:** Requirements now match OMRRA. Numbers are required on the front and on each side of the belly pan (numbers on top of the tail no longer count for side requirements).  Add numbers to your belly pan to be compliant.

**FU/Open SBK/SS:** Due to member feedback regarding the added costs of extra races, open classes will remain exclusively in the WMRRA Title Championship and will no longer be included in the Club Championship.

**750SS / 750 Amateur / 750 Novice:** Added to accommodate the growing number of large-bore twins on the market.  Hopefully, we can attract more new riders for this growing market segment.

**F40 Sportbike Next Gen:** There’s been some demand for a smaller category for the over-40 classes for a few years. The reason we don’t have an F40 for every size bike is simply scheduling conflicts with existing championship classes. There’s only so much time in a day.  We’re giving this a try to see what the turnout will be.

**Fire Extinguishers:** Every rider (rather than one per pit) must now have a visible 10BC fire extinguisher (standard household/kitchen size, 10BC is a measurement of coverage rather than weight and previous requirement of weight and coverage conflicted and was confusing).

**Racing Leathers:** Leathers no longer need to be brought to tech inspection at any point during the season.

**Race Starting Procedure:** The grid board has been eliminated. Check the rulebook to learn the updated procedure.

**Red Flag:** The red flag procedure has changed.  Please be sure and read the section on Red Flag procedures; it is critical to your races. The procedure is very similar to OMRRAs, but due to our tracks being different, there are specifics that you need to know. Riders not following the rule and exiting will be DNF’d.  Ignorance of the procedure does not excuse you from it.

**Concussion Protocol:** Riders that are determined by medical to have a concussion will not be permitted to continue riding at a race round.

**Additionally, I made a significant amount of organization changes in the Rulebook, combining multiple rules into single categories of procedures and to make sections easier to identify.**

- Louis Stevenson, WMRRA Race Referee
